# The Analog Schism: Voice Bible (DRAFT, derived from World Bible v1.0)

## What the bible already sounds like
- **Register:** briefing-room clarity. Declarative, concrete, institutional. Systems are described by what they control and what they cost ("controls power and water; nominally neutral, actually the most quietly powerful").
- **Human scale:** the bible grounds big systems in one ordinary citizen's day (section 4): rent, medicine, flagged accounts. Keep doing this. A rule is only real once it costs someone something.
- **Moral texture:** nobody is the villain in a clean way. Factions fracture (four Weaver currents), corporations perform unity in public and undermine each other in private. Write rivals with reasons.
- **Terms of art:** capitalised and stable (the Crown, the Midstack, the Works, the Sub-Tiers, the Blight, the Grey Hour, Node 04). Never vary a locked term for style.

## Naming patterns found in canon (observed, not invented)
| Kind | Pattern | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Corporations | one evocative word, often botanical, mineral or an old trade word; a plain function noun after it | Halcyon Systems, Vireo Biomed, Ferrous Works Authority, Aldergate Media & Education, Thorne Logistics |
| Factions and currents | "The" + role or craft noun | The Archivists, The Cut, The Splice Councils, The Cradle Faithful |
| Places and layers | physical position or function, no proper name | the Crown, the Midstack, the Works, the Sub-Tiers, the Dead Grid |
| Events | a plain time word plus a plain noun | the Grey Hour, the Blight |
| People | short plain given name + surname (two protagonists share the surname Venn) | Mara Venn, Adrian Rook, Priya Osei |

## Proposed rules for new writing (DRAFT, needs approval)
1. A new corporation, faction or place follows the pattern in its row above. Function words stay plain.
2. A new person gets one given name and one surname. One family, one surname. People from the Crown and people from the Sub-Tiers should not sound interchangeable: that is a design choice for Josh, not something I will decide.
3. Every new named thing is added to a registry (stable id, display name, source section) before it appears in prose.
4. Every named NPC carries: a want that is not "fix the city", a limit, one thing they will not discuss, what they know and do not know about Node 04, and a reason their tone changes.
5. The model never names things. The engine supplies names from the registry or from canon.
6. No imitation of living authors or other franchises' proper nouns.
